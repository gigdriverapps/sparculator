Privacy Policy for Sparculator

Effective Date: November 25, 2026
Developer: gigDriverApps
Application Package: com.gigdriverapps.sparculator

1. Overview

Sparculator ("the App") is a delivery wait-time and hourly-rate calculator built for independent gig delivery drivers. Your privacy is important to us. Sparculator does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or device data with external servers or third parties.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide core functionality—including syncing your active countdown timer and rate inputs between your phone and Android Auto—the App saves configuration and session state strictly in local, app-scoped storage on your device:

  • Calculation Inputs: Offer pay, target hourly rate, estimated trip duration, wait pay rate, and grace period settings.
  • Timer State: Active timer start time, target expiration timestamp, and remaining duration so your countdown continues accurately in the background and on your car display.

All stored data remains exclusively on your device and is permanently removed when you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.

3. Android Permissions Used

Sparculator requests only the minimum system permissions required to operate your wait-time countdown and alerts:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) & Full-Screen Intent (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT): Displays your active countdown and walk-away deadline alert on your phone and Android Auto screen.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE) & Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK): Delivers tactile vibration alerts when your wait-time deadline expires while the device screen is locked or off.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE): Keeps your user-initiated wait timer accurate while switching apps or driving.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Sparculator contains no advertising SDKs, no behavioral analytics or telemetry libraries, and no external cloud accounts.

5. Children's Privacy

Sparculator is designed for adult gig-economy drivers and is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
